About Pretty Little Plans

A lifestyle guide for making more of your weekends, nights out and everyday plans.

For people who always want something to look forward to.

“If it's fun, I'm probably going.”

Pretty Little Plans is my place to share the restaurants, events, neighborhoods, nights out, sporting events, concerts and trips that make life feel fuller.

I'm based in Minneapolis, and I love finding the places and experiences I'd actually recommend to a friend — then turning those ideas into practical, pretty plans you can use too.

Think of this as the friend who sends you the restaurant link, remembers when tickets go on sale and already has a plan for Saturday night.

The idea

Plan less. Experience more.

The goal is simple: make it easier to discover something good, put it on the calendar and actually go do it.
