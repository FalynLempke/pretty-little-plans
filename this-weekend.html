This Weekend in Minneapolis ✨

September 4–7 · Labor Day Weekend

Concerts, fair days, free finds and a few very good reasons to leave the house.

THE BIG PLAN

Last Weekend of the Minnesota State Fair

Labor Day weekend is your last chance for a 2026 fair day. Go hungry, wander without rushing, catch some free music and stay for the nighttime energy.

Plan your fair day
FRIDAY NIGHT · SEPT 4

Pick Your Concert

Friday has options: HARDY with McCoy Moore at the State Fair Grandstand at 7 PM, Isaiah Rashad at The Fillmore at 6:30 PM, or Dermot Kennedy at The Armory at 8 PM.

Pretty Little Plan: Dinner downtown → Dermot Kennedy at The Armory → one last cocktail before heading home.

Browse Friday shows
SATURDAY NIGHT · SEPT 5

TLC + Salt-N-Pepa + En Vogue

This is the nostalgia night. The IT'S ICONIC tour hits the State Fair Grandstand at 7 PM — an easy centerpiece for a full fair day with friends.

Pretty Little Plan: Late-afternoon fair food → Grandstand show → fireworks and one more walk through the fair.

FREE + EASY

Minnehaha Park Market

Friday from 4–8 PM: make it a low-key start to the long weekend with the Minnehaha Park Market, then stay around the park or head downtown for the night.
